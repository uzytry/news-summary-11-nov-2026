US-mediated talks between Russia and Ukraine ended in Abu Dhabi with agreement on a major prisoner release, Reuters reported. Jared Kushner called it the biggest such release of the war, with numbers to be finalized Thursday. The first talks in nine months left the territory question unresolved; Kushner and Steve Witkoff visit Moscow Thursday to meet Putin.

At COP31 in Antalya, UN chief Antonio Guterres proposed a 'climate justice compact' and urged windfall taxes on fossil fuels: nine oil giants earned $95 billion in a quarter against $600 million for the Loss and Damage Fund, UN News reported.

Russia's ambassador to the US, Alexander Darchiev, died suddenly in Moscow aged 66, the foreign ministry said; spokesperson Maria Zakharova cited a chronic illness, Reuters reported.

Spain's first general strike in 14 years brought millions out over housing ahead of a 29 November snap election. Average rent is €1,176 a month, nearly 99% of a young person's wage, Reuters reported.

Hoax bomb threats to some 6,300 Italian schools forced hundreds to evacuate; similar emails hit schools in Scotland, Northern Ireland and Ireland. Prime Minister Meloni said the threats were fake, Reuters reported.

Kenya discharged its last Ebola patient, starting a 42-day countdown to Ebola-free status around 23 December; the outbreak saw nine cases, seven recoveries and two deaths, Reuters reported.

A bus plowed into 32 vehicles in Seongnam, South Korea, injuring 57, nine seriously, Reuters reported, citing the fire agency. The driver said the brakes had failed.

The Financial Times reported bankers may price Anthropic's IPO above its $70-$78 range after heavy demand, lifting its valuation past $1.5 trillion. It should trade on Nasdaq as 'ANT' on 19 November.

Astronomers found a candidate supermassive neutron star, an unseen object of about 2.2 solar masses tugging on a red giant in Pisces, in Science Advances. Most weigh about 1.4 solar masses, Scientific American reported.
